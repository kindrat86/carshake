Choose the guide for the handover in front of you

These guides turn vehicle-condition documentation into a practical sequence. Start with the moment you are dealing with now: before a valet handover, during rental pickup, after parking, at a fleet shift change, or while preparing a claim. Follow one guide from beginning to end instead of mixing unrelated steps.

Use wide photos to show location and close photos to show detail. Preserve the original files, note when responsibility changed, and keep communication factual. The guides explain documentation practices; they do not guarantee that a valet company, insurer, rental agency, or court will accept a claim.

How-to guide FAQ

Which guide should I read first?

Choose the guide that matches the current stage. If no damage has occurred, start with prevention and handover documentation. If damage is already disputed, start with the dispute, claim, or letter guide.

Do I need special camera equipment?

No. A phone camera is enough for the workflows described here when the vehicle is visible, the lens is clean, and the photos include both overview and detail views.

Are these guides legal or insurance advice?

No. They provide practical documentation guidance. Requirements vary, so check the relevant contract, insurer instructions, and local rules for your situation.
