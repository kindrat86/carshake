CarShake vs Ravin AI (2026): Consumer Handover Proof vs Enterprise Claims AI

TL;DR: These tools are not really competitors. Ravin AI is a B2B computer-vision platform that helps insurers, fleets, and vehicle businesses automate inspections and claims. CarShake is a free consumer app that gives drivers timestamped, GPS-verified photo proof when someone else handles their car. If you are a driver worried about a valet or rental damage dispute, CarShake is built for you. If you are an insurer building claims automation, Ravin is.

What Ravin AI actually is

Ravin AI (Ravin AI, Inc., Austin, TX) sells AI-powered vehicle inspection and claims-automation tooling to businesses. Its stack, per ravin.ai:

  • RAVIN Inspect — a web-based mobile capture app: AI-guided 360° walkaround scanning with no app install required, used so customers or tow drivers can submit photos into a claims flow.
  • RAVIN Eye — a review dashboard for adjusters: 360° views, damage disclosures, condition reports with damage types, severity, and cost, plus APIs to export into claim systems.
  • RAVIN AutoScan — fixed CCTV-style cameras (sets of four or more) that scan moving vehicles at up to 30 km/h, flagging dents, scratches, and misaligned or replaced panels on site.
  • DeepDetect / RepairIQ — its patented detection AI and repair-estimation engine, built on billions of vehicle records, recommending repair and settlement options.

The key point for comparison: everything Ravin ships is sold business-to-business and wired into claims, fleet, and remarketing platforms. There is no consumer product a driver can just download and use before handing keys to a valet.

Where Ravin AI wins

Be clear-eyed about this: for its market, Ravin is the more sophisticated product.

  • Automated damage detection and classification at fleet scale, trained on billions of vehicle records
  • Repair cost estimates and total-loss recommendations delivered into claim-management systems
  • Drive-through CCTV scanning (AutoScan) — 4+ fixed cameras catching vehicles moving up to 30 km/h, no driver effort at all
  • APIs and exportable condition reports that plug into existing claims, fleet, and remarketing platforms
  • Remote 360° review (Eye) for adjusters triaging claims from anywhere

If you run an insurance claims operation, a rental fleet, or an auction house and need machine-generated condition reports at volume, Ravin is purpose-built for that job. CarShake is not a claims-automation platform and does not try to be.

Where CarShake wins (for drivers)

  • Free and instant — open the web app, scan, done. No enterprise contract, demo call, or integration project.
  • Dispute-first design — the before/after protocol exists to answer one question: was this damage there when I handed over the keys?
  • Evidence you can present yourself — GPS coordinates, device + server timestamps on every photo, SHA-256 hashes for tamper-evidence, and a QR receipt marking the custody transfer.
  • Works everywhere — any smartphone browser; no cameras to install, no IT project.

Ravin cannot help you at a restaurant valet stand tonight. Its tooling sits on the business side of a claim you would be filing against that valet company. CarShake is what you use at the curb.

Using both together

The tools are complementary. A rental operator may run Ravin AutoScan at the lot gate for its own condition logs, while each customer independently runs CarShake at pickup and return. When a damage dispute arises, the driver's timestamped handover record and the operator's scan logs can be compared — which usually settles the argument quickly, in whoever's favor the evidence actually falls.

Pricing reality

CarShake's core scan-and-receipt workflow is free; the optional Shield+ plan costs $2.97/month and adds PDF reports and priority support. Ravin AI publishes no pricing — it is sold through demo requests and custom enterprise quotes, which is normal for insurance-infrastructure vendors. Frequently Asked Questions

Is CarShake better than Ravin AI?

Neither tool is universally better — they solve different problems. Ravin AI builds AI inspection and claims-automation infrastructure sold to insurers, fleets, and vehicle businesses. CarShake gives individual drivers and small operators free, timestamped photo proof at vehicle handovers. For a driver facing a valet or rental damage dispute, CarShake fits. For an insurer automating damage assessment, Ravin fits.

What does Ravin AI cost?

Ravin AI does not publish pricing. It sells to insurers, fleets, and vehicle businesses through demos and custom quotes requested on ravin.ai. CarShake is free, with an optional Shield+ plan at $2.97/month for PDF reports and priority support.

Can I use Ravin AI as a driver to prove valet damage?

Not directly. Ravin AI is a B2B platform — its mobile capture (RAVIN Inspect), review dashboard (RAVIN Eye), and CCTV scanning (RAVIN AutoScan) are licensed to businesses and integrated into claims workflows. There is no consumer product an individual driver can download. CarShake is the consumer-side option: you capture before and after scans yourself and get timestamped proof that ties any new damage to the handover window.

Can I use both CarShake and Ravin AI?

Yes, and they complement each other. A rental company or fleet operator might use Ravin for automated condition reports on every vehicle, while each customer uses CarShake for an independent handover record at pickup and return.

Does CarShake use AI like Ravin AI does?

Both use AI, but differently. CarShake compares your before and after scans to flag new damage inside a consumer workflow. Ravin's DeepDetect AI detects and classifies damage type and severity, and its RepairIQ produces repair cost estimates and total-loss recommendations for claims systems.

Which produces stronger evidence for a damage dispute?

For a consumer dispute — valet, rental return, body shop — CarShake's GPS-verified, dual-timestamped, hash-verified photo record plus QR handover receipt creates a tight chain of custody you can present yourself. Ravin's condition reports are designed for enterprise claims processing, not for a driver negotiating at a valet stand.
