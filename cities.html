Valet Damage Documentation by City

Parking conditions differ by city, but a sound vehicle handoff routine stays the same. Before a valet, garage attendant, rental counter, or repair shop takes your keys, make a complete visual record. Repeat it when you get the vehicle back.

A quick condition-record routine

Start with wide photos of the front, rear, and both sides. Then capture the wheels, windshield, mirrors, and any existing damage from close range. Keep the original files and record the location and handoff time. At return, repeat the same sequence before you leave the lot. A consistent process makes it easier to show what changed, if anything, during the handoff.

These guides add context for drivers in major cities. They are general educational resources, not legal advice or a statement about any specific parking operator.
