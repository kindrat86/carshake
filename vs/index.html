Comparison FAQ

Does CarShake claim to replace every platform listed here?

No. The compared products serve overlapping but different jobs. Some are designed for enterprise vehicle inspections or insurance workflows. CarShake focuses on creating a clear before-and-after handover record.

Are the comparisons based only on feature counts?

No. The pages consider intended user, setup, workflow, and the kind of condition record produced. Feature counts alone can hide a major difference in who a product is built for.

How should I verify a comparison before buying?

Check the vendor's current documentation, request a demonstration if the workflow is business-critical, and test the capture process with the people who will actually use it. Product capabilities and pricing can change.
