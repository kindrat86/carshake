Why event venues valet in Pennsylvania generates so many damage disputes

Pennsylvania event venues operate under conditions that make damage disputes almost inevitable: peak-load valet surges, contract (not in-house) valet crews with high staff churn, and dark overflow lots. The disputes surface most during event load-in and load-out rushes. Without a timestamped before-state record, the event venue's valet operator can (and often does) claim the damage was pre-existing — and the driver has no way to prove otherwise.

How Pennsylvania bailment law fits in

When you hand your keys to a valet in Pennsylvania, you've created a bailment — the event venue (or its valet contractor) takes temporary custody of your vehicle and is expected to return it in the condition it was received. In practice, valet operators often try to disclaim liability for cosmetic damage, and the dispute almost always comes down to one question: can you prove the car's condition before handover? CarShake's timestamped, GPS-verified, SHA-256-hashed photos plus the QR handover receipt are exactly that proof.
