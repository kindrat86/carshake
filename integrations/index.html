Insurance Claim Documentation Guides

When vehicle damage is disputed, an insurer may ask for photos, dates, repair estimates, and a clear account of what happened. These guides explain how a condition record can help you prepare for that conversation.

Document first, contact the right party second

Start by preserving the original photos, recording the handoff location and time, and writing a short factual timeline. If the vehicle was with a valet, rental business, garage, or another driver, keep the receipt or handoff record as well. Avoid editing the original images. If a claim is appropriate, follow the insurer's own instructions and deadlines.

CarShake does not submit insurance claims or represent an insurer. It helps drivers make a consistent before-and-after record that can be shared with the relevant business, insurer, or advisor when needed.

Build a usable record

For each handoff, capture a complete walkaround before and after, note any existing damage, and keep any receipt from the location. For a detailed process, read how to document pre-existing damage or start a free condition record.
