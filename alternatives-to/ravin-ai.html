But all of it is licensed business-to-business and wired into claims, fleet, and remarketing platforms. Pricing is unpublished; you request a demo.

So an individual driver cannot choose Ravin in the first place — and a rental counter or valet stand is not going to run an enterprise scan workflow for you at the curb. The real question is: what do you use, right now, to protect yourself at handover? That is the gap CarShake fills.
